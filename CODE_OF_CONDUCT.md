# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be direct, be kind, assume good faith. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not welcome.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## Reporting

Open a private security advisory or contact the maintainers. Reports are handled
confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may
ban repeat offenders.
